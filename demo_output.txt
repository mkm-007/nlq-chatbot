Q: How many employees are in Engineering?
SQL: SELECT COUNT(*) AS count FROM employees WHERE department = 'Engineering'
A: 2
---
Q: List employees in HR
SQL: SELECT name, department, salary FROM employees WHERE department = 'HR'
A: name=Meera, department=HR, salary=70000.0; name=Jon, department=HR, salary=72000.0
---
Q: What is the average salary in Engineering?
SQL: SELECT AVG(salary) AS avg_salary FROM employees WHERE department = 'Engineering'
A: 87500.0
